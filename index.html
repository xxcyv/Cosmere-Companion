Cosmere Reading Order Companion

Which book should you read next?

Line Styles and Meaning

Required: Must read to understand the story. Recommended: Avoid spoilers or understand subplots or major references. Nice to have: Understand minor references or bigger eastereggs.

Controls

Moving the Diagram

You can move the diagram sideways by dragging it with the cursor.

Selection Filtering

Left-click: Shows dependency overview for a specific book, including what leads to it.

Right-click: Helps find your next read by showing direct right-side dependencies of books you already read. Right-click multiple books to combine.

Copying Titles

Alt + Click: Copies the selected book title to the clipboard.
